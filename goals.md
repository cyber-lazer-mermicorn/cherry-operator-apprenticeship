# Goals

## Primary Goal
Progressive, evidence-based operator capability toward AI-commerce and technical work.

## Curriculum
01-digital-operations → 02-research-verification → 03-github → 04-structured-data → 05-ai-graphics → 06-commerce → 07-automation → 08-mcp → 09-quality-assurance → 10-deployment

## First Proof
Completion of at least one module with a real, reviewable artifact (e.g., Module 04 structured product record + listing + sanitized case study).

## Success
A visible ladder of real artifacts that demonstrates readiness for higher-responsibility operator work.
